This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how CODA Resources Vietnam LLC ("we," "us," or "our") uses them on our website codaresources-vietnam.com, and your choices regarding their use.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. (Compliance Basis: Legitimate Interest)
  • Analytical/Performance: To analyze how visitors use our website, monitor website performance, and improve user experience. (Compliance Basis: Consent)
  • Functionality: To remember choices you make (such as language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features. (Compliance Basis: Consent)

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that set cookies to help us analyze our website traffic and user behavior.

A. Google Analytics

  • Purpose: Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how visitors use our site. We use the information to compile reports and to help us improve the site. The cookies collect information in an anonymous form, including the number of visitors to the site, where visitors have come to the site from, and the pages they visited.
  • Data Collected: IP address (anonymized), device information, pages visited, time spent on site, referring source.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt-out of Google Analytics without affecting how you visit our site by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

B. Microsoft Clarity

  • Purpose: Microsoft Clarity is a user behavior analytics tool that helps us see how users interact with our website through session replays, heatmaps, and usage metrics. This helps us identify areas for improvement.
  • Data Collected: Interaction data (mouse movements, clicks, scrolls), device information, pages visited.
  • Opt-Out: You can manage your preferences for Microsoft Clarity tracking through your browser settings or by visiting the Microsoft Privacy Dashboard.

4. Your Consent and Management

In compliance with the EU's ePrivacy Directive (Cookie Law) the Vietnamese Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations, we require your explicit consent for the use of non-essential cookies (Analytical and Functionality).

  • Consent: When you first visit our website, a cookie banner will appear, allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Withdrawal: You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the cookies from your browser settings. You can also adjust your browser settings to refuse all or some cookies.

Refusing non-essential cookies does not affect your ability to access the website.

5. Anti-fraud and Cross-Border Data Transfers

In cases where necessary to ensure system security and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities, we may use strictly necessary cookies and related technical data to detect and prevent misuse of the website. Such processing is carried out within the necessary scope and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Information collected through cookies may, in some cases, be transmitted and processed on data storage systems located outside the territory of Vietnam. Any such cross-border data transfer is carried out in accordance with applicable legal regulations, and appropriate technical and organizational safeguards are applied to ensure compliance with Vietnam's data protection law, and relevant international standards.

6. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information on our use of cookies.
